## 2. The "Institutional Adoption" (認養) Cycle
Based on deep-dive research into top gainers, a consistent 3-stage institutional cycle was identified:

### Stage 1: Stealth Accumulation (隱形佈局)
- **Technical**: VCP / Tight Consolidation.
- **Chip**: Trust (投信) holding moves from **0% -> 1~3%**. 
- **Signal**: First 3-5 days of consecutive buying after a long period of zero activity.

### Stage 2: Participation & Acceleration (主升段)
- **Technical**: Volume breakout, upward trend.
- **Chip**: Trust holding scales to **5~10%**.
- **Signature**: High "Absorption Ratio" (Daily Net Buy > 2% of total daily volume).
- **Peer Effect**: If multiple peers in the same sector see similar accumulation (e.g., Heavy Electric in 2023), the conviction is Highest.

### Stage 3: Distribution & Exhaustion (末升/作帳結束)
- **Chip**: Trust holding reaches **12~15%**. 
- **Signal**: Large "Sell" orders appearing after a parabolic move or at quarter-end.

## 3. Quantitative Thresholds for Strategy Refinement
- **Adoption Start**: Trust holding > 0.5%.
- **Strong Conviction**: Trust holding > 3% AND 5-day buying streak.
- **Danger Zone**: Trust holding > 12-15% (potential profit-taking crash).

## 4. Conclusion
The "Chip Layer" is NOT just about high holding; it's about **Rate of Change** in institutional ownership during the VCP contraction phase. Stocks that transition from "No Institutional Interest" to "Adoption Start" during a VCP pattern have the highest probability of becoming 2x+ gainers.

The plan will be refined to focus on these **Momentum-based Chip Signals** (0.5% -> 3% transition).
